Three Goals

Every concept and skill in the course calibrates against these three axes.

🛡

Safe from Bugs

Correct today, correct in the face of an unknown future. Catch bugs before they happen with static checking, thorough testing, and invariants.

Safe from Bugs
📖

Easy to Understand

Communicate clearly with future programmers — including future you. Use precise specifications and abstraction to make code self-documenting.

Easy to Understand
🔄

Ready for Change

Design with no intention of rewriting. Use representation independence and modularity so change happens in exactly one place.

Ready for Change
