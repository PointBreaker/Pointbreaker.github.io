Exam 3：积分

第三次期中考试，闭卷，覆盖 Lecture 18–24：定积分定义、FTC1/FTC2、换元法、面积、旋转体体积（圆盘/柱壳）、功/平均值/概率、梯形与 Simpson 数值积分。本导读使用 review 讲义与 pre-exam 练习，不发布完整评分试卷。

类型：期中考试 3（闭卷） 日期：2006-11-07 13:05–13:55 覆盖：Lecture 18–24

考试范围

Exam 3 是积分学的第一次综合考，重点在「用积分建模」与「计算方法」。考点：

  • FTC1/FTC2：\(\displaystyle\frac{d}{dx}\int_a^x f(t)\,dt=f(x)\)，\(\displaystyle\int_a^b f=F(b)-F(a)\)。
  • 换元法：\(\displaystyle\int f(g(x))g'(x)\,dx=\int f(u)\,du\)。
  • 面积：直角坐标、参数、极坐标。
  • 体积：圆盘/垫圈 vs. 柱壳，选择 \(dx\) 或 \(dy\) 积分。
  • 应用：功（变力沿路径做功）、平均值、概率密度。
  • 数值积分：梯形法则、Simpson 法则及其误差。

核心公式速查

体积计算决策树

绕 \(y\) 轴旋转：函数 \(y=f(x)\)，通常柱壳 \(2\pi x\cdot f(x)\,dx\) 更方便。

绕 \(x\) 轴旋转：函数 \(x=g(y)\)，通常柱壳 \(2\pi y\cdot g(y)\,dy\)；函数 \(y=f(x)\) 则圆盘 \(\pi f(x)^2\,dx\)。

关键：决定对哪个变量积分（\(dx\) 还是 \(dy\)），然后其余量系统确定。

数值积分公式

  • 梯形法则（\(n\) 等份，步长 \(h\)）：\(\displaystyle T_n=\frac{h}{2}\left(y_0+2y_1+\cdots+2y_{n-1}+y_n\right)\)
  • Simpson 法则（\(n\) 偶数）：\(\displaystyle S_n=\frac{h}{3}\left(y_0+4y_1+2y_2+4y_3+\cdots+4y_{n-1}+y_n\right)\)

功的计算

变力 \(F(x)\) 沿 \(x\) 轴从 \(a\) 到 \(b\) 做功：\(\displaystyle W=\int_a^b F(x)\,dx\)。

典型例子：单摆重力做功 \(W=\int_0^{\theta_0} mg\sin\theta\cdot L\,d\theta = mgL(1-\cos\theta_0)\)——只与下降高度有关，与路径无关。

平均值

连续平均值：\(\displaystyle \bar{f}=\frac{1}{b-a}\int_a^b f(x)\,dx\)。

代表性练习（来自 review 与 pre-exam 材料）

类型 A：FTC2 与变限积分（prexam3a/4）

设 \(F(x)=\int_0^x(3+\sin t)\,dt\)：

  • 证明 \(F(1)\ge 2\)：因为 \(3+\sin t\ge 2\) 在 \([0,1]\) 成立，积分 \(\ge 2\cdot 1=2\)。
  • 凹凸性：\(F'(x)=3+\sin x\)，\(F''(x)=\cos x\)。在 \((0,1)\)，\(\cos x>0\)，所以 \(F\) 上凸（concave up）。
  • 换元：\(\int_1^2(3+\sin 2t)\,dt\) 用 \(u=2t\) 转化为 \(\frac12\int_2^4(3+\sin u)\,du\)，用 \(F\) 值表示。

类型 B：黎曼和定义（prexam3a/2）

用「上黎曼和（外接矩形）」直接算 \(\displaystyle\int_0^1 x\,dx\)：

\[U_n=\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{i}{n}\cdot\frac1n=\frac1{n^2}\sum_{i=1}^n i=\frac1{n^2}\cdot\frac{n(n+1)}2=\frac{n+1}{2n}\]

取极限 \(n\to\infty\) 得 \(1/2\)。这道题让你再次体验「没有 FTC 时算积分」的繁琐。

类型 C：旋转体体积（prexam3a/6,7）

「花瓶由 \(y=\sqrt{x}\) 绕 \(y\) 轴旋转，\(x\in[0,a]\)」：圆盘法（对 \(y\)）：

\[V=\int_0^{\sqrt{a}}\pi(y^2)^2\,dy=\pi\int_0^{\sqrt{a}}y^4\,dy=\frac{\pi a^{5/2}}{5}\]

「圆锥冰淇淋」：圆锥 \(y\) 轴截面 \(x=\sqrt{6-y}\)（由 \(y=6-x^2\)），顶部被平面截。用柱壳法更方便：对 \(x\) 积分，柱壳半径 \(x\)，高 = 顶部抛物线减底部圆锥。

类型 D：平均值（prexam3a/8）

「半径 \(a\) 的四分之一圆内接矩形，\(x\) 在 \([0,a]\) 均匀随机，求面积平均值」：

\[\bar{A}=\frac1a\int_0^a x\sqrt{a^2-x^2}\,dx\]

令 \(u=a^2-x^2\)，积分得 \(\frac1a\cdot\frac{a^3}{3}=\frac{a^2}{3}\)。

类型 E：数值积分（prexam3a/9）

用梯形法则与 Simpson 法则（\(n=2\)）估算 \(\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^6 x\,dx\)。注意 Simpson 法则对三次以下多项式精确，这里被积函数不是多项式，但 \(n=2\) 仍可给出近似。

类型 F：连续复利模型（prexam3a/3）

连续复利 \(A(t)=A_0e^{rt}\)，每日连续存款速率 \(k\)（年率）。一年后账户总额：

\[\text{Total}=\int_0^1 k\,e^{r(1-t)}\,dt\]

建模理由：在时刻 \(t\) 存入的 \(k\,dt\) 到年末增长因子为 \(e^{r(1-t)}\)。

常见陷阱

  • 体积方法选错：绕 \(y\) 轴时若函数是 \(y=f(x)\)，别硬用圆盘（需要反函数），优先考虑柱壳。
  • 数值积分系数记混：梯形端点系数 1，内部 2；Simpson 端点 1，奇数下标 4，偶数下标 2。
  • 功的积分变量：单摆功 = 力 × 弧长微元 \(L\,d\theta\)，不是 \(dx\)。
  • FTC2 换元忘改限：\(\int_a^b f(g(x))g'(x)\,dx\) 换元 \(u=g(x)\) 时，上限变为 \(g(b)\)、下限变为 \(g(a)\)。
  • 平均值忘除区间长度：\(\bar{f}=\frac{1}{b-a}\int_a^b f\)，不要只算积分。

知识检查

问题 1：\(y=f(x)\)（\(x\in[a,b]\)）绕 \(y\) 轴的柱壳体积元是？

问题 2：Simpson 法则在 \([x_0,x_2]\)（中点 \(x_1\)，半长 \(h\)）的公式是？

问题 3：\(\displaystyle\frac{d}{dx}\int_0^x(3+\sin t)\,dt\) 等于？

自测小结

FTC

FTC2 把积分转为原函数差；FTC2 把变限积分求导转为被积函数。

体积

圆盘 vs. 柱壳：由旋转轴与函数形式决定积分变量。

应用

功、平均值、概率——积分是「累积求和」的统一语言。

数值积分

梯形（线性） vs. Simpson（抛物线），后者精度更高。

Problem 1 Evaluate integrals Definite integrals · Substitution · Trigonometric integrals

Evaluate the following definite integrals.

  1. a) integral from 0 to 1 of x/(1 + 3x^2) dx
  2. b) integral from -pi/3 to pi/2 of cos^3 x sin 2x dx

公式与已知条件

  • sin 2x = 2 sin x cos x
  • Standard antiderivative techniques
解题入口

For (a), use a u-substitution on the denominator. For (b), express sin 2x in terms of sin x and cos x, then use substitution on the resulting trigonometric integrand.

完成后检查

  • Verify limits of integration are applied correctly
  • Check sign of result against the integrand's behavior on the interval

Problem 2 Integral from definition (upper sums) Definition of the Riemann integral · Upper sums · Limit of a sum

Evaluate integral from 0 to 1 of x dx directly from its definition as the limit of a sum. Use upper sums (circumscribed rectangles). You can use the formula sum_{i=1}^{n} i = n(n + 1)/2.

公式与已知条件

  • sum_{i=1}^{n} i = n(n + 1)/2
  • Upper sum uses right endpoints on [0,1]
解题入口

Partition [0,1] into n equal subintervals of width Delta x = 1/n. Use right endpoints x_i = i/n to form the upper sum, then take the limit as n -> infinity.

完成后检查

  • Confirm the known value integral from 0 to 1 of x dx = 1/2 is recovered
  • Verify the sum formula is applied correctly

Problem 3 Continuous compounding integral setup Continuous compounding · Definite integral modeling · Future value of a continuous deposit stream

A bank gives interest at rate r, compounded continuously, so an amount A_0 grows to A(t) = A_0 e^{rt} after t years. You make a daily deposit at the constant annual rate k (over time Delta t you deposit k Delta t dollars). Set up a definite integral (with reasoning) for the amount in the account at the end of one year. Do not evaluate the integral.

公式与已知条件

  • A(t) = A_0 e^{rt}
  • Deposit over Delta t is k Delta t
解题入口

Accumulate each infinitesimal deposit k dt made at time t over the year [0,1], each growing continuously for the remaining time (1 - t). Sum (integrate) these contributions.

完成后检查

  • Confirm the integral has limits 0 to 1
  • Verify units: k has units of dollars/year, dt in years

Problem 4 Properties of a function defined by an integral Fundamental Theorem of Calculus · Monotonicity and bounds · Convexity/concavity via second derivative · Change of variables in integrals

Consider F(x) = integral from 0 to x of (3 + sin t) dt. Without finding an explicit formula for F(x):

  1. a) (5 points) Show that F(1) >= 2.
  2. b) (5 points) Determine whether F(x) is convex (concave up) or concave (concave down) on 0 < x < 1; show work or give reasoning.
  3. c) (10 points) Give, in terms of values of F(x), the value of integral from 1 to 2 of (3 + sin 2t) dt.

公式与已知条件

  • F'(x) = 3 + sin x (FTC)
  • F''(x) = cos x
  • Substitution u = 2t relates the integral in (c) to F
解题入口

(a) Bound the integrand below on [0,1] and use monotonicity of the integral. (b) Use F''(x) and its sign on (0,1). (c) Use a substitution to express the integral in terms of F.

完成后检查

  • (a) Confirm lower bound reasoning
  • (b) F''(x) = cos x > 0 on (0,1)
  • (c) Verify the substitution factor

Problem 5 Find constant and integrand from FTC Fundamental Theorem of Calculus · Differentiation of integral functions

If integral from 0 to x of f(t) dt = e^{2x} cos x + c, find the value of the constant c and the function f(t).

公式与已知条件

  • FTC: d/dx integral from 0 to x of f(t) dt = f(x)
  • Evaluate at x = 0 to find c
解题入口

Differentiate both sides to find f(x); evaluate the original equation at x = 0 to determine c.

完成后检查

  • Confirm c = -1 from the x = 0 evaluation
  • Verify f(x) reproduces the derivative

Problem 6 Volume of a vase (rotation about y-axis) Volumes of revolution · Horizontal slicing · Disk/washer method

A glass vase is the solid obtained by rotating about the y-axis the area in the first quadrant lying over [0, a] and under the graph of y = x. By slicing horizontally, determine how much glass it contains.

公式与已知条件

  • Curve: y = x (so x = y)
  • Horizontal slices from y = 0 to y = a
解题入口

Express x as a function of y (x = y). Each horizontal slice at height y is a disk of radius x = y; integrate pi(radius)^2 with respect to y from 0 to a.

完成后检查

  • Confirm radius = y
  • Check integration limits 0 to a

Problem 7 Ice cream cone volume (cylindrical shells) Volumes of revolution · Cylindrical shells method

A right circular cone has height 5 and base radius 1; it is over-filled with ice cream in the usual way. Place the cone with vertex at the origin and axis along the positive y-axis. The cross-section containing the x-axis has top given by the parabola y = 6 - x^2. The full filled cone is obtained by rotating this cross-section about the y-axis. Find the volume of the ice cream. (Suggestion: use cylindrical shells.)

公式与已知条件

  • Shell method: V = 2pi integral of x * (height) dx
  • Parabolic top: y = 6 - x^2
  • Cone geometry: height 5, base radius 1
解题入口

Use cylindrical shells with radius x, height given by the vertical extent from the cone surface to the parabola y = 6 - x^2. Determine the correct x-limits from the geometry.

完成后检查

  • Verify x-limits from cone/parabola intersection
  • Confirm shell formula includes 2pi factor

Problem 8 Average area of inscribed rectangles Average value of a function · Quarter-circle geometry

Rectangles are inscribed in a quarter-circle of radius a, with the point x chosen randomly on [0, a]. Find the average value of their area.

公式与已知条件

  • Quarter-circle: y = sqrt(a^2 - x^2)
  • Area of rectangle at position x: A(x) = x * sqrt(a^2 - x^2)
  • Average value formula: (1/(b-a)) integral of A(x) dx
解题入口

Express the area of an inscribed rectangle as a function of x, then compute its average value over x in [0, a] using the average value formula.

完成后检查

  • Confirm A(x) formula from quarter-circle geometry
  • Verify average value denominator is a

Problem 9 Trapezoidal and Simpson's rule approximation Numerical integration · Trapezoidal rule · Simpson's rule

Find the approximate value of the integral from 0 to pi/2 of sin^6 x dx using the trapezoidal rule and also Simpson's rule, taking n = 2 (dividing the interval into two equal subintervals).

  1. Trapezoidal rule approximation
  2. Simpson's rule approximation

公式与已知条件

  • n = 2, interval [0, pi/2], so Delta x = pi/4
  • Nodes: x_0 = 0, x_1 = pi/4, x_2 = pi/2
  • Trapezoidal: (Delta x/2)[f(x_0) + 2f(x_1) + f(x_2)]
  • Simpson's: (Delta x/3)[f(x_0) + 4f(x_1) + f(x_2)]
解题入口

Evaluate sin^6 x at x = 0, pi/4, pi/2. Apply the trapezoidal and Simpson's rule formulas with Delta x = pi/4.

完成后检查

  • Confirm Delta x = pi/4
  • Verify function values at the three nodes
