Problem Set 8：极坐标、洛必达、级数与泰勒级数

课程的最后一次常规作业，分 8A（Lecture 30–31：参数方程、弧长、极坐标面积、悬链线）与 8B（Lecture 34–37：洛必达、反常积分、级数收敛、Gamma 函数递推、球面帽优化）。内容跨度大，是期末总复习的绝佳素材。

类型：Problem Set 8（8A + 8B） 截止：2006-12-08 13:55 覆盖：Lecture 31, 34, 35, 36, 37（8A 含 Lecture 30）

学习目标

  • 把参数曲线 \(x=a\cos^k t,\ y=a\sin^k t\) 化为直角坐标方程，并计算弧长。
  • 用极坐标面积公式 \(\frac12\int r^2\,d\theta\) 计算区域面积，并与直角坐标结果对照。
  • 用洛必达法则判断 \(\int_0^\infty x^n e^{-x}\,dx\) 的收敛性，并用分部积分推导 Gamma 递推 \(A(n+1)=nA(n)\)。
  • 结合球冠体积与表面积，优化肥皂泡形状（最小表面积问题）。

主题梳理与示例

1. 参数曲线 \(x=a\cos^k t,\ y=a\sin^k t\)（8A/1）

取 \(k=1\)：\(x=a\cos t,\ y=a\sin t\)，满足 \(x^2+y^2=a^2\)（圆），弧长 \(=a\cdot\pi/2\)。

取 \(k=2\)：\(x=a\cos^2 t=a(1+\cos 2t)/2\)，\(y=a\sin^2 t=a(1-\cos 2t)/2\)，满足 \(x+y=a\)（线段），但参数只描出 \(0\le x,y\le a\) 的一段；弧长可直接由对称性得 \(a\)。

一般 \(k\)：弧长积分 \(\displaystyle L=\int_0^{\pi/2}\sqrt{(dx/dt)^2+(dy/dt)^2}\,dt\)，代入求值并与 \(k=1,2\) 的几何结果对照。

2. 悬链线与悬链面（8A/2）

双曲函数定义 \(\cosh x=\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}{2}\)，\(\sinh x=\frac{e^x-e^{-x}}{2}\)。基本性质：\((\sinh x)'=\cosh x\)，\((\cosh x)'=\sinh x\)，\(\cosh^2 x-\sinh^2 x=1\)。

参数曲线 \(x=\cosh t,\ y=\sinh t\) 满足 \(x^2-y^2=1\)（双曲线）。悬链线 \(y=\cosh x\) 的弧长：

\[s=\int_0^{x_1}\sqrt{1+\sinh^2 x}\,dx=\int_0^{x_1}\cosh x\,dx=\sinh x_1\]

悬链面（绕 \(x\) 轴旋转）的表面积：

\[A=\int 2\pi y\,ds=\int_0^{x_1}2\pi\cosh x\cdot\cosh x\,dx=2\pi\int_0^{x_1}\cosh^2 x\,dx\]

悬链面是连接两个圆盘的「最小表面积」形状——肥皂膜实验的经典结果，与最速降线、等周问题同为变分法起源。

3. 极坐标面积（8A/3）

极坐标面积公式：\(\displaystyle A=\frac12\int_{\theta_1}^{\theta_2}r^2\,d\theta\)。

曲线 \(r=1/(1+\sin\theta)\) 化为直角坐标：\(r+r\sin\theta=1\)，即 \(\sqrt{x^2+y^2}+y=1\)，整理得 \(x^2=1-2y\)（开口向下的抛物线）。

区域 \(0\le r\le 1/(1+\sin\theta)\)，\(0\le\theta\le\pi\) 的面积：

\[A=\frac12\int_0^\pi\frac{1}{(1+\sin\theta)^2}\,d\theta\]

令 \(u=\theta-\pi/2\)，用 \(1+\cos u=2\cos^2(u/2)\) 化简。这个例子说明：极坐标面积公式在处理「绕焦点扫过的面积」时比直角坐标方便（开普勒定律的背景）。

4. Gamma 函数与反常积分（8B/1）

用洛必达法则：\(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}x^m e^{-x}=0\)（指数衰减比任何幂增长快）。由此用比较判别法证明 \(\int_0^\infty x^n e^{-x}\,dx\) 对 \(n\ge 0\) 收敛。

记 \(A(n)=\int_0^\infty x^n e^{-x}\,dx\)。分部积分：

\[A(n+1)=\int_0^\infty x^{n+1}e^{-x}\,dx=\left[-x^{n+1}e^{-x}\right]_0^\infty+(n+1)\int_0^\infty x^n e^{-x}\,dx=(n+1)A(n)\]

边界项在 \(\infty\) 处为 0 正是用了洛必达的结果。又 \(A(0)=1\)，所以 \(A(n)=n!\)。

5. 肥皂泡形状优化（8B/2）

球冠体积 \(V=\pi(rh^2-h^3/3)\)，其中 \(r\) 是球半径，\(h\) 是冠高。球冠表面积（只算曲面）：

\[S=2\pi rh\]

在体积 \(V\) 固定下最小化 \(S\)：由 \(V\) 的公式把 \(r\) 表为 \(h\) 的函数，代入 \(S\)，对 \(h\) 求导令为零，得到 \(h/r=1\)，即半球。所以最优肥皂泡是半球。

常见坑

  • 参数曲线弧长积分限：\(t\) 从 \(0\) 到 \(\pi/2\) 只描出第一象限部分。
  • 极坐标面积漏乘 \(1/2\)：面积元是 \(\frac12 r^2\,d\theta\)，不是 \(r^2\,d\theta\)。
  • 洛必达使用条件：必须是不定式 \(0/0\) 或 \(\infty/\infty\)，且导数极限存在。
  • 分部积分边界项：\(\left[-x^{n+1}e^{-x}\right]_0^\infty\) 在 \(\infty\) 处为 0 需要洛必达保证。

自测小结

参数弧长

\(ds=\sqrt{(dx/dt)^2+(dy/dt)^2}\,dt\)，注意参数范围。

极坐标面积

\(\frac12\int r^2\,d\theta\)，注意 \(\theta\) 范围避免重复计数。

洛必达 + 反常积分

指数衰减快于幂增长，保证边界项为 0。

优化

球冠体积固定 → 半球表面积最小。

Problem 1 Parametric curves x = a cos^k t, y = a sin^k t Parametric equations · Arclength

Analyze the parametric curve x = a cos^k t, y = a sin^k t.

  1. a) Find the algebraic equation in x and y for the curve x = a cos^k t, y = a sin^k t. Draw the portion of the curve 0 ≤ t ≤ π/2 in the three cases k = 1, k = 2, k = 3.
  2. b) Without calculation, find the arclength in the cases k = 1 and k = 2.
  3. c) Find a definite integral formula for the length of the curve for general k. Then evaluate the integral in the three cases k = 1, k = 2, and k = 3. (Your answer in the first two cases should match what you found in part (b), but the calculation takes more time.)

公式与已知条件

  • x = a cos^k t, y = a sin^k t
  • ds = √((dx/dt)² + (dy/dt)²) dt
解题入口

For (a), eliminate t using cos²t + sin²t = 1. For (c), compute ds = √((dx/dt)² + (dy/dt)²) dt and integrate.

完成后检查

  • Answers for k=1,2 in (c) should match part (b).

Problem 2 Hyperbolic functions and the catenary Hyperbolic functions · Parametric equations · Arclength · Surface area of revolution

Work with hyperbolic sine and cosine, the catenary, and the catenoid.

  1. a) Show that: i) d/dx sinh x = cosh x and d/dx cosh x = sinh x; ii) cosh²x = 1 + sinh²x; iii) cosh²x = (1 + cosh 2x)/2.
  2. b) What curve is described parametrically by x = cosh t, y = sinh t? (Give the equation and its name.)
  3. c) The curve y = cosh x is known as a catenary. i) Sketch the curve. ii) Find its arclength from the lowest point to the point (x₁, cosh x₁) for a fixed x₁ > 0.
  4. d) Find the area of the surface of revolution formed by revolving the portion of the curve from part (c) around the x-axis. This surface is known as a catenoid.

公式与已知条件

  • cosh x = (e^x + e^{-x})/2
  • sinh x = (e^x - e^{-x})/2
  • d/dx sinh x = cosh x
  • d/dx cosh x = sinh x
  • cosh²x = 1 + sinh²x
  • cosh²x = (1 + cosh 2x)/2
解题入口

Use exponential definitions. For arclength and surface area, use the standard formulas.

完成后检查

  • Derivatives should match the identities; arclength and surface area should be positive.

Problem 1 L'Hospital's rule and improper integrals L'Hospital's rule · Improper integrals · Limit comparison · Integration by parts · Gamma function

Use L'Hospital's rule and limit comparison to analyze convergence of improper integrals and derive a recurrence for A(n).

  1. a) Use L'Hospital's rule to evaluate lim_{x→∞} x^m e^{-x}.
  2. b) Use part (a) and limit comparison to show that the improper integral ∫_0^∞ x^n e^{-x} dx converges for n ≥ 0. (Do not integrate by parts.)
  3. c) Denote A(n) = ∫_0^∞ x^n e^{-x} dx. Use integration by parts to find the constant c_n for which A(n+1) = c_n A(n). (Explain what happens at the infinite limit using part (a).)
  4. d) Find A(0) and deduce from part (c) the formula for A(n), n = 0, 1, 2, ...
  5. e) (optional; 5 pts extra credit) Show that the improper integral representing A(-1/2) converges and evaluate it using a change of variables.

公式与已知条件

  • A(n) = ∫_0^∞ x^n e^{-x} dx
  • A(n+1) = c_n A(n)
解题入口

For (a), rewrite as x^m/e^x and apply L'Hospital. For (c), integrate by parts with u = x^{n+1}.

完成后检查

  • Recurrence should yield A(n) = n! for integer n.

Problem 2 Spherical cap and soap bubble Solids of revolution · Spherical cap · Surface area · Optimization

Find volume and surface area of a spherical cap and determine the shape of a soap bubble minimizing surface area for fixed volume.

  1. a) Show that the volume V of the solid of revolution enclosed on the top by a spherical cap of radius r of height h and underneath by a horizontal plane is V = π(rh² - h³/3). Draw a picture. Double-check this answer in the three cases: h = 0; h = r; h = 2r. Assume 0 ≤ h ≤ 2r.
  2. b) Find the surface area of the spherical cap of radius r and height h. Double-check your answer by evaluating it in the three cases h = 0, h = r and h = 2r.
  3. c) Suppose the bubble is a portion of a sphere enclosing a fixed volume V, and its surface area is as small as possible (curved surface only, not counting the flat bottom = table). Find the minimizing shape by finding h/r.

公式与已知条件

  • V = π(rh² - h³/3)
  • 0 ≤ h ≤ 2r
解题入口

For (a) and (b), revolve an appropriate circle segment. For (c), minimize surface area subject to fixed volume.

完成后检查

  • Volume and area formulas should reduce correctly at h=0, h=r, h=2r.
