本作业把导数的三大应用——相关变化率、中值定理、牛顿法——集中在一张 PS 里，并第一次系统涉及微分方程与不等式的证明。GPS 灵敏度问题在 PS1 首次登场后，在这里用隐式微分给出精确答案。

学习目标

  • 用隐式微分处理相关变化率问题，并解释线性近似 \(\Delta L/\Delta h \le dL/dh\) 的几何含义。
  • 掌握牛顿法 \(x_{n+1}=x_n-f(x_n)/f'(x_n)\) 的迭代格式、收敛判断与误差估计。
  • 用中值定理（MVT）证明不等式，并求极值问题的端点最值。
  • 用相关变化率推导星形线（hypocycloid）的切线包络。

主题梳理与示例

1. 极值问题（Part II/1）

梯子靠墙问题（4.3/28）：设梯子脚距墙 \(x\)，列出到达高度关于 \(x\) 的函数，求最小值并检查端点。这类问题容易忽略定义域端点——18.01 反复强调「极值可能出现在端点」。

3. 测量灵敏度精确化（Part II/3）

PS1 中的 GPS 关系 \(L^2+20000^2=h^2\)。隐式微分：

\[2L\frac{dL}{dh}=2h\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{dL}{dh}=\frac{h}{L}=\frac{h}{\sqrt{h^2-20000^2}}\]

与 PS1 的差商 \(\Delta L/\Delta h\) 比较：当 \(\Delta h>0\) 且导数在左端点取值时，\(\Delta L/\Delta h\le dL/dh\)，这是因为 \(dL/dh\) 随 \(h\) 增大而增大（上凸函数）。

一维情形（一维行星 + 两卫星）：由两个观测联立求解位置，并讨论 \(x_1x_1\) 两种顺序。

这类「反问题」是工程中的常见模式：通过多个含噪声的观测推断未知量，并用导数估计误差传播。

4. 抛物面镜与角度灵敏度（Part II/4）

抛物线 \(y=-\frac14+x^2\)，焦点在原点。光线沿 \(x=a\) 竖直入射点 \((a,b)\)，反射到原点，反射光线与 \(x\) 轴夹角 \(\theta\)：

\[\tan\theta=\frac{b}{a}=\frac{-1/4+a^2}{a}\]

隐式微分求 \(d\theta/da\)，再利用 \(\theta=-\pi/6\)、测量精度 \(10^{-3}\) 弧度反推 \(a\) 的不确定度。

5. 牛顿法求 \(9^{1/3}\)（Part II/5）

求 \(x^3=9\) 的正根，迭代函数 \(f(x)=x^3-9\)，牛顿格式：

\[x_{n+1}=x_n-\frac{x_n^3-9}{3x_n^2}=\frac{2x_n^3+9}{3x_n^2}\]

取 \(x_0=2\)，迭代直到稳定到 6 位有效数字。注意每一步 \(x_k\) 都大于精确值 \(9^{1/3}\)（因为牛顿法对凸函数从左侧逼近），且序列单调递减。

第 (c) 小题用 \(9=8(1+1/8)\) 与二次逼近 \((1+x)^{1/3}\approx 1+x/3-x^2/9\) 估算 \(9^{1/3}=2(1+1/8)^{1/3}\)。

6. 星形线的包络（Part II/6，额外学分）

长度为 1 的梯子从竖直滑到水平，包络线即所有位置的「公共切线」形成的曲线。设两位置 \((0,y_1)\to(x_1,0)\) 与 \((0,y_2)\to(x_2,0)\)，交点：

\[x_3=\frac{x_1y_2-x_2y_1}{y_2-y_1+x_1-x_2}\quad\text{（对称式）}\]

令 \(x_2\to x_1\)、用 \(x_1^2+y_1^2=1\) 的隐式微分求极限 \((X,Y)\)，最终得到 \(X^{2/3}+Y^{2/3}=1\)。这是一道极具综合性的难题，把相关变化率、极限、隐式微分融为一体。

常见坑

  • 极值漏检端点：梯子问题中 \(x\) 的定义域是闭区间，端点可能给出最值。
  • 牛顿法收敛判断不清：要明确写出「何时达到计算器精度」，并列出每步数值。
  • 隐式微分后忘记用原方程化简：第 3 题 \(dL/dh=h/L\)，而 \(L\) 要代回 \(\sqrt{h^2-20000^2}\)。
  • 相关变化率中符号与单位：注意 \(\Delta h>0\) 与 \(\Delta h<0\) 对应的不等式方向不同。

自测小结

相关变化率

隐式微分 + 原方程化简，注意几何/物理意义。

牛顿法

\(x_{n+1}=x_n-f/f'\)，凸函数时单调递减地从右侧逼近。

中值定理

用 MVT 把函数值差转化为导数乘积，证明不等式。

包络

曲线族的公共切线形成包络，取交点极限可得方程。

Problem 3 Sensitivity of measurement, revisited Implicit differentiation · Sensitivity of measurement · Linear approximation · Error propagation · Geometry of satellite ranging

Uses the relationship from PS1/Part II problem 2 to explore sensitivity via implicit differentiation.

  1. a) Recall that in problem 2, PS1/Part II, L^2 + 20,000^2 = h^2. Use implicit differentiation to calculate dL/dh. Compare the linear approximation dL/dh to the error ΔL/Δh computed in examples on PS1. Explain why ΔL/Δh ≤ dL/dh if the derivative is evaluated at the left endpoint of the interval of uncertainty (Δh > 0). In what range of values of h is it true that |ΔL| ≤ 2|Δh|?
  2. b) Suppose Planet Quirk is not only flat but one-dimensional (a straight line). Several satellites are at height 20,000 km. Satellite 1 is directly above x1 ± 10^(-10) at distance h1 = 21,000 ± 10^(-2); satellite 2 is directly above x2 ± 10^(-10) at distance h2 = 52,000 ± 10^(-2). Where are you and to what accuracy? Hint: Consider separately the cases x1 < x2 and x2 > x1.
  3. c) Express dL/dh in terms of the angle between the line of sight to the satellite and the horizontal from the person on the ground. (When expressed using the line-of-sight angle, the formula also works for a curved planet like Earth.)

公式与已知条件

  • L^2 + 20,000^2 = h^2
  • h1 = 21,000 ± 10^(-2)
  • h2 = 52,000 ± 10^(-2)
  • Position uncertainties: ± 10^(-10)
解题入口

Differentiate the Pythagometric relation implicitly; for (b) use the two distance equations to solve for your position and propagate errors; for (c) use right-triangle trigonometry.

完成后检查

  • For (a), verify the inequality direction and the h-range algebraically.
  • For (b), check both orderings of x1 and x2.
  • For (c), confirm the formula reduces to the flat case and generalizes to curved geometry.

Problem 4 More sensitivity of measurement (parabolic mirror) Implicit differentiation · Related rates · Parabolic mirror / focus geometry · Error estimation

Consider a parabolic mirror with equation y = -1/4 + x^2 and focus at the origin. A ray of light traveling vertically down along x = a hits the mirror at (a, b) where b = -1/4 + a^2 and goes to the origin along a ray at angle θ measured from the positive x-axis.

  1. a) Find the formula for tan θ in terms of a and b, and calculate dθ/da using implicit differentiation. Express your answer in terms of a and θ.
  2. b) If the telescope records a star at θ = -π/6 and the measurement is accurate to 10^(-3) radians, use part (a) to estimate the location of the star in the variable a.
  3. c) (optional; no credit) Solve for a as a function of θ alone and double-check your answers to parts (a) and (b).

公式与已知条件

  • Mirror: y = -1/4 + x^2
  • Hit point: (a, b) with b = -1/4 + a^2
  • Focus at origin
  • θ = -π/6 with accuracy ± 10^(-3) radians
解题入口

Relate θ to the slope of the ray from (a, b) to the origin; differentiate implicitly; for (b) use dθ ≈ (dθ/da) da.

完成后检查

  • For (a), verify dimensions and sign conventions.
  • For (b), confirm the estimated a is consistent with θ = -π/6.
  • For (c), cross-check via explicit solution if attempted.

Problem 5 Newton's method Newton's method · Iterative approximation · Quadratic approximation / Taylor expansion

Compute the cube root of 9 using Newton's method.

  1. a) Compute 9^(1/3) to 6 significant figures using Newton's method. Give the general formula, list numerical values starting with x0 = 2. At what iteration k does the method surpass the accuracy of your calculator or computer? Display answers to the accuracy of your calculator or computer.
  2. b) For each step xk, k = 0, 1, …, say whether the value is i) larger or smaller than 9^(1/3); ii) larger or smaller than the preceding value x(k-1). Illustrate on the graph of x^3 - 9 why this is so.
  3. c) Find a quadratic approximation to 9^(1/3) and estimate the difference between the quadratic approximation and the exact answer. Hint: use 9 = 8(1 + 1/8).

公式与已知条件

  • Target: 9^(1/3)
  • x0 = 2
  • Rewrite: 9 = 8(1 + 1/8)
解题入口

Use Newton's method on f(x) = x^3 - 9; for (c) expand 9^(1/3) = 2(1 + 1/8)^(1/3) via binomial/Taylor expansion.

完成后检查

  • For (a), confirm convergence to 6 significant figures.
  • For (b), compare each iterate to the true root and the previous iterate.
  • For (c), verify the quadratic approximation error estimate is reasonable.

Problem 6 Hypocycloid, again (extra credit) Hypocycloid · Intersection of lines · Implicit differentiation · Related rates · Limit of swept region boundary

Derive the equation for the hypocycloid of Problem 2 from the sweeping-out property directly. Model the first quadrant as the region to the right of a wall with ground as positive x-axis and wall as positive y-axis. A unit ladder placed vertically against the wall slides to horizontal while its top is dragged down to the origin, sweeping out a region.

  1. a) Suppose L1 is the segment from (0, y1) to (x1, 0) and L2 from (0, y2) to (x2, 0). Find the formula for the intersection point (x3, y3) of the two segments, involving all four parameters x1, x2, y1, y2. Simplify as much as possible. Optional checks: i) if y2 = 0 then x3 = x1; ii) symmetry under swapping x and y; iii) denominator zero when lines are parallel; iv) rescaling all variables by c leaves formula unchanged, so the numerator degree is one greater than the denominator.
  2. b) Write the equation involving x2 and y2 expressing that ladder L2 has length one. Let L1 be fixed with L2 tending to L1: x2 = x1 + Δx, y2 = y1 + Δy. Use implicit differentiation (related rates) to find lim_(Δx→0) Δy/Δx as a function of x1 and y1.
  3. c) Substitute x2 = x1 + Δx and y2 = y1 + Δy into part (a)'s formula for x3 and use part (b) to compute X = lim_(x2→x1) x3 = lim_(Δx→0) x3. Simplify and deduce Y = lim_(x2→x1) y3 by symmetry.
  4. d) Show that X^(2/3) + Y^(2/3) = 1.

公式与已知条件

  • Ladder length = 1
  • L1: segment (0, y1) to (x1, 0)
  • L2: segment (0, y2) to (x2, 0)
  • x2 = x1 + Δx, y2 = y1 + Δy
解题入口

For (a) derive intersection from two line equations; for (b) differentiate the length constraint; for (c) take the limit using the related-rate slope; for (d) combine the parametric expressions.

完成后检查

  • For (a), verify the four optional consistency checks.
  • For (b), confirm the limit matches the slope from implicit differentiation of x1^2 + y1^2 = 1.
  • For (d), verify the resulting relation is exactly the hypocycloid equation.
