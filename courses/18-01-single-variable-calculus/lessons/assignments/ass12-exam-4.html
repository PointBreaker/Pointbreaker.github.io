Exam 4：积分技巧

考试范围

Exam 4 是积分技巧的专考。随卷公式表给出基本三角恒等式与少数积分公式，但不会告诉你「该用什么技巧」——技巧选择仍是考点。

  • 三角换元：\(\sqrt{a^2-x^2}\to x=a\sin u\)，\(\sqrt{a^2+x^2}\to x=a\tan u\)，\(\sqrt{x^2-a^2}\to x=a\sec u\)。
  • 三角积分：\(\int\sin^m x\cos^n x\,dx\)、\(\int\tan^n x\,dx\) 递推、\(\int\sec x\,dx\)。
  • 部分分式：有理函数 \(P/Q\) 系统积分——先长除（若 \(\deg P\ge\deg Q\)），再分解，逐项积分。
  • 分部积分：\(\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du\)，选 \(u\) 的 LIATE 原则。
  • 弧长与面积：\(ds=\sqrt{1+(dy/dx)^2}\,dx\)，旋转面 \(2\pi y\,ds\)（绕 \(x\) 轴）。
  • 极坐标面积：\(\frac12\int r^2\,d\theta\)，注意 \(\theta\) 范围避免重复计数。

三角恒等式

  • \(\sin^2 x+\cos^2 x=1\)，\(\sec^2 x=\tan^2 x+1\)

  • \(\cos 2x=\cos^2 x-\sin^2 x\)，\(\sin 2x=2\sin x\cos x\)

导数公式

  • \(\displaystyle\frac{d}{dx}\tan x=\sec^2 x\)，\(\displaystyle\frac{d}{dx}\sec x=\sec x\tan x\)
  • \(\displaystyle\frac{d}{dx}\tan^{-1}x=\frac{1}{1+x^2}\)，\(\displaystyle\frac{d}{dx}\sin^{-1}x=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\)

积分公式

  • \(\displaystyle\int\tan x\,dx=-\ln(\cos x)+C\)
  • \(\displaystyle\int\sec x\,dx=\ln(\sec x+\tan x)+C\)

技巧决策与示例

三角换元后的「回代」

做完三角换元得到 \(\int f(\theta)\,d\theta\) 后，必须用直角三角形把 \(\theta\) 函数换回 \(x\)。例如 \(x=a\sin u\) 时，对边 \(=x\)、斜边 \(=a\)、邻边 \(=\sqrt{a^2-x^2}\)，所以 \(\sin u=x/a\)、\(\cos u=\sqrt{a^2-x^2}/a\)。

有理函数积分系统流程

例：\(\displaystyle\int\frac{x^3}{x^2-2x+1}\,dx\)。

  1. 长除：\(\deg P=3\ge\deg Q=2\)，做除法：\(\displaystyle\frac{x^3}{x^2-2x+1}=x+2+\frac{3x-2}{(x-1)^2}\)。
  2. 部分分式：\(\displaystyle\frac{3x-2}{(x-1)^2}=\frac{3}{x-1}+\frac{1}{(x-1)^2}\)。
  3. 逐项积分：\(\displaystyle\int(x+2)\,dx+\int\frac{3}{x-1}\,dx+\int\frac{1}{(x-1)^2}\,dx\)。

对于分母含不可约二次因子（如 \(x^2+9\)），部分分式项 \(\frac{Cx+D}{x^2+9}\) 的积分仍需三角换元 \(x=3\tan u\)。

分部积分的 LIATE 原则

选 \(u\) 的优先级：Logarithmic（对数）→ Inverse trig（反三角）→ Algebraic（代数）→ Trig（三角）→ Exponential（指数）。例如 \(\int x^2 e^x\,dx\)：\(u=x^2\)（代数），\(dv=e^x\,dx\)。

弧长与旋转面面积

参数曲线 \(x=x(t),\ y=y(t)\) 的弧长：

\[s=\int_{t_0}^{t_1}\sqrt{(dx/dt)^2+(dy/dt)^2}\,dt\]

绕 \(x\) 轴旋转的表面积：\(A=\int 2\pi y\,ds\)。绕 \(y\) 轴则 \(2\pi x\,ds\)。注意：公式要求你已知 \(y=y(x)\) 或 \(x=x(y)\) 的形式，否则要调整。

极坐标面积的范围陷阱

求 \(\frac12\int r^2\,d\theta\) 时，\(\theta\) 的范围必须使曲线恰好扫过区域一次。例如 \(r=\cos\theta\) 的圆在 \(\theta\in[-\pi/2,\pi/2]\) 扫过一次；若取 \([0,2\pi]\) 会重复计数。

常见陷阱

  • 三角换元后忘回代：最终答案必须是 \(x\) 的函数，不能停留在 \(\theta\)。
  • 有理函数不先长除：\(\deg P\ge\deg Q\) 时直接部分分式会出错。
  • 部分分式漏项：\((x-1)^2\) 要拆成 \(\frac{A}{x-1}+\frac{B}{(x-1)^2}\)，不能只写一项。
  • 分部积分选错 \(u\)：\(\int x\ln x\,dx\) 选 \(u=\ln x\)（对数优先），不是 \(u=x\)。
  • 极坐标面积 \(\theta\) 范围：画图确认扫过区域，避免重复或遗漏。

Problem 1 Trig substitution and trig integrals Trigonometric substitution · Trigonometric integrals · Right-triangle back-substitution

Integrate by trigonometric substitution; evaluate the trig integral and work back to the original variable using a right triangle (trig(trig^{-1})).

  1. a) For sqrt(a^2 - x^2), use x = a sin u, dx = a cos u du.
  2. b) For sqrt(a^2 + x^2), use x = a tan u, dx = a sec^2 u du.
  3. c) For sqrt(x^2 - a^2), use x = a sec u, dx = a sec u tan u du.

公式与已知条件

  • x = a sin u for a^2 - x^2
  • x = a tan u for a^2 + x^2
  • x = a sec u for x^2 - a^2
  • Printed: sin^2 x + cos^2 x = 1; sec^2 x = tan^2 x + 1
  • Printed: sin^2 x = (1/2)(1 - cos 2x); cos^2 x = (1/2)(1 + cos 2x)
  • Printed: cos 2x = cos^2 x - sin^2 x; sin 2x = 2 sin x cos x
解题入口

Choose the substitution matching the form of the radical. After integrating in u, draw a right triangle to convert back to x.

完成后检查

  • Confirm the correct substitution for each radical form
  • Verify back-substitution via triangle

Problem 2 Partial fractions (rational functions) Partial fractions · Polynomial long division · Completing the square · Rational function integration

Integrate rational functions P/Q by partial fractions. If deg(P) >= deg(Q), perform long division first. Factor Q completely into linear and quadratic factors and decompose.

  1. Example form: (3x^2 + 1)/[(x - 1)(x + 2)^2(x^2 + 9)] = A/(x - 1) + B_1/(x + 2) + B_2/(x + 2)^2 + (Cx + D)/(x^2 + 9)
  2. Terms like D/(x^2 + 9) may be integrated using trig substitution x = 3 tan u.

公式与已知条件

  • Decomposition includes terms A/(x - a)^k for linear factors and (ax + b)/(quadratic)^k for irreducible quadratics
  • Completing the square: x^2 + 4x + 13 = (x + 2)^2 + 9 = u^2 + a^2, u = x + 2, a = 3
  • Long division when deg(P) >= deg(Q): P/Q = P_1 + R/Q with deg(R) < deg(Q)
解题入口

Check degree of numerator vs denominator first (long division if needed). Factor denominator; set up partial fraction form; solve for coefficients (cover-up method where applicable). Integrate each piece.

完成后检查

  • Verify deg(R) < deg(Q) after division
  • Confirm coefficient values satisfy the decomposition

来源：exams/0202fa3893049a6a502c4f7079eca657_exam4_review.pdf · Lecture p.1; Handout pp.1-2

Problem 3 Integration by parts Integration by parts

Apply integration by parts: integral of u dv = uv - integral of v du (definite: integral from a to b of u dv = [uv]_a^b - integral from a to b of v du). Use when v du is simpler than u dv (often when du is simpler than u).

公式与已知条件

  • integral of u dv = uv - integral of v du
  • Definite form: integral from a to b of u dv = [uv]_a^b - integral from a to b of v du
解题入口

Choose u to be the part that simplifies when differentiated (e.g., ln x, polynomial, inverse trig). Choose dv to be the remainder (easily integrable).

完成后检查

  • Confirm the new integral of v du is simpler than the original

Problem 4 Arc length and surface area of revolution Arc length · Surface area of revolution

Compute arc length and surface area of revolution using the appropriate ds expressions.

  1. Arc length: ds = sqrt(dx^2 + dy^2); ds = sqrt(1 + (dy/dx)^2) dx; ds = sqrt((dx/dt)^2 + (dy/dt)^2) dt; ds = sqrt((dx/dy)^2 + 1) dy
  2. Surface area about x-axis: 2pi y ds = 2pi y sqrt(1 + (dy/dx)^2) dx (needs y = y(x))
  3. Surface area about y-axis: 2pi x ds = 2pi x sqrt((dx/dy)^2 + 1) dy (needs x = x(y))

公式与已知条件

  • ds = sqrt(1 + (dy/dx)^2) dx
  • ds = sqrt((dx/dt)^2 + (dy/dt)^2) dt
  • S_x = 2pi integral of y sqrt(1 + (dy/dx)^2) dx
  • S_y = 2pi integral of x sqrt((dx/dy)^2 + 1) dy
解题入口

Identify the variable of integration and choose the correct ds formula. For surface area, multiply by 2pi times the radius (the coordinate perpendicular to the axis).

完成后检查

  • Confirm correct radius term (y for x-axis, x for y-axis)
  • Verify ds matches the integration variable

Problem 5 Polar coordinates Polar coordinates · Coordinate conversion · Polar curve sketching

Work with polar coordinates: x = r cos theta, y = r sin theta (and r = sqrt(x^2 + y^2), theta = tan^{-1}(y/x)). Find polar equations from (x,y) equations by substitution, and sketch curves given in polar form.

  1. a) Find the polar equation for a curve from its (x,y) equation by substitution.
  2. b) Sketch curves given in polar coordinates and understand the range of theta (often in preparation for integration).

公式与已知条件

  • x = r cos theta
  • y = r sin theta
  • r = sqrt(x^2 + y^2)
  • theta = tan^{-1}(y/x)
解题入口

Substitute x = r cos theta and y = r sin theta into Cartesian equations to obtain polar form. For sketching, consider symmetry and the appropriate theta-range to trace the curve once.

完成后检查

  • Verify substitution yields a valid polar equation
  • Confirm theta-range avoids double-counting or missing regions

Problem 6 Area in polar coordinates Polar area integration

Compute area enclosed by a polar curve using A = integral from theta_1 to theta_2 of (1/2) r^2 d theta. Pay attention to the range of theta to avoid double-counting or missing regions.

公式与已知条件

  • A = integral from theta_1 to theta_2 of (1/2) r^2 d theta
解题入口

Determine the theta-interval that traces the region exactly once. Integrate (1/2) r(theta)^2 over that interval.

完成后检查

  • Confirm theta-limits trace the region once without overlap

Problem 7 Parametric equations (arc length) Parametric equations · Arc length in parametric form

Student question: What do we need to know about parametric equations? Answer: use ds = sqrt((dx/dt)^2 + (dy/dt)^2) dt. Example: given x(t) = t^4 and y(t) = 1 + t, find the arc length s.

  1. Example: x(t) = t^4, y(t) = 1 + t; ds = sqrt((4t^3)^2 + (1)^2) dt, then integrate with respect to t.

公式与已知条件

  • ds = sqrt((dx/dt)^2 + (dy/dt)^2) dt
  • Example derivatives: dx/dt = 4t^3, dy/dt = 1
解题入口

Compute dx/dt and dy/dt, form ds = sqrt((dx/dt)^2 + (dy/dt)^2) dt, then integrate over the given t-interval (limits to be specified in a concrete problem).

完成后检查

  • Confirm derivatives are correct
  • Verify the ds expression
