主题梳理与示例

1. 链式法则与高阶导数（2A/2）

计算 \(\frac{d}{dx}\tan^3(x^4)\)：从外到内逐层剥离：

\[\frac{d}{dx}\tan^3(x^4)=3\tan^2(x^4)\cdot\sec^2(x^4)\cdot 4x^3\]

第二小题 \(\frac{d}{dy}(\sin^2y\cos^2y)\) 要求两种做法：直接乘法法则，以及先改写为 \(f(2y)\) 再利用链式法则，验证结果一致。这是训练「化简优先于求导」的好例子。

2. 乘积法则的高阶推广（2A/3）

已知 \(y=uv\)，验证：

\[y''=u''v+2u'v'+uv'',\qquad y'''=u'''v+3u''v'+3u'v''+uv'''\]

系数 \(1,2,1\) 与 \(1,3,3,1\) 恰好是二项式系数——这暗示着 Leibniz 公式 \((uv)^{(n)}=\sum_{k=0}^n\binom{n}{k}u^{(n-k)}v^{(k)}\)。

3. 反余弦导数与符号（2A/4）

设 \(y=\cos^{-1}x\)，则 \(\cos y=x\)，\(0\le y\le\pi\)。隐式微分：

\[-\sin y\cdot y'=1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y'=-\frac{1}{\sin y}\]

由 \(\sin y\ge 0\)（\(y\in[0,\pi]\)），\(\sin y=\sqrt{1-x^2}\)，于是：

\[\frac{d}{dx}\cos^{-1}x=-\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\]

符号是关键：\(\sin y\) 在 \([0,\pi]\) 非负，所以开根号取正。对比 \(\sin^{-1}x\) 的导数取 \(+1/\sqrt{1-x^2}\)，两者符号相反，因此 \(\frac{d}{dx}\cos^{-1}x+\frac{d}{dx}\sin^{-1}x=0\)，这与 \(\cos^{-1}x+\sin^{-1}x=\pi/2\) 吻合。

4. 球面帽面积的逼近（2B/1）

球半径 \(R\)，截面圆半径 \(r\)，球心到截面距离 \(\sqrt{R^2-r^2}\)，所以球冠高：

\[h=R-\sqrt{R^2-r^2}\]

球冠面积（已知球面带面积公式 \(2\pi Rh\)）：

\[A=2\pi Rh=2\pi R^2\left(1-\sqrt{1-(r/R)^2}\right)\]

令 \(x=-(r/R)^2\)，则 \(\sqrt{1-(r/R)^2}=\sqrt{1+x}\)。用线性与二次逼近：

\[(1+x)^{1/2}\approx 1+\frac{x}{2},\qquad (1+x)^{1/2}\approx 1+\frac{x}{2}-\frac{x^2}{8}\]

代入得：线性逼近给出 \(A\approx \pi r^2\)（熟悉的圆面积），二次逼近给出更精确的值。高尔夫球题（\(R=1.5\) cm，凹槽半径 \(r=0.15\) cm，100 个）要求你比较「平面近似」「二次逼近」「精确公式」三种答案，判断竞赛要求的四舍五入精度下哪种足够。

这类「近似何时可用」的建模题是 18.01 的核心能力：先用泰勒展开得到误差量级，再代入具体数值验证。

5. 函数 \(1/(1+x^2)\) 的图像族（2B/2）

在同一坐标系下画 \(f(x)=1/(1+x^2)\)、\(f'(x)=-2x/(1+x^2)^2\)、\(f''(x)=(6x^2-2)/(1+x^2)^3\)，标出极值点 \((0,1)\) 与拐点 \((\pm 1/\sqrt{3},\,3/4)\)，并用竖直线把特殊点对齐到导数图像上。这是训练「从 \(f\) 的图像读出 \(f'\) 的符号与 \(f''\) 的拐点」的标准练习。

常见坑

  • 反三角函数开根号忘判号：\(\cos^{-1}x\) 的值域决定 \(\sin y\ge 0\)。
  • 链式法则漏层：\(\tan^3(x^4)\) 有三层——幂、\(\tan\)、\(x^4\)，逐层相乘。
  • 逼近时忽略 \(x\) 的尺度：\((1+x)^{1/2}\) 逼近只在 \(|x|\ll 1\) 有效；高尔夫球题中 \(r/R=0.1\) 足够小。
  • 混淆球冠高与截面到球心距离：\(h=R-\sqrt{R^2-r^2}\)，不是 \(r\)。

自测小结

隐函数求导

反三角函数导数靠隐式微分 + 值域判号。

对数微分

多因子乘积/幂先取对数，化乘为加再求导。

逼近

\((1+x)^{1/2}\) 的线性/二次逼近给出球冠面积的粗略/精细估计。

Leibniz 公式

\((uv)^{(n)}\) 的系数是二项式系数。

Problem 2A-4 Inverse cosine derivative implicit differentiation · inverse trigonometric functions · derivatives

Inverse trigonometric derivatives. (Fri; 4 pts)

  1. a) The function cos^{−1} x is the inverse of cos θ on 0 ≤ θ ≤ π. Use implicit differentiation to derive the formula for (d/dx) cos^{−1} x. Pay particular attention to the sign of the square root. (See the book or lecture for the case of the inverse of sine.) (3 pts)
  2. b) Without calculation, explain why (d/dx) cos^{−1} x + (d/dx) sin^{−1} x = 0. (1 pt)
解题入口

(a) Let y = cos^{-1} x so cos y = x. Differentiate: -sin y dy/dx = 1. On [0,π], sin y ≥ 0 so sin y = +sqrt(1-x^2). (b) Since cos^{-1} x + sin^{-1} x = π/2 (constant), differentiating gives the sum of derivatives is zero.

完成后检查

  • (a) positive square root used because sin y ≥ 0 on [0,π]
  • (b) constant-sum identity used correctly

Problem 2B-1 Golf balls (spherical caps) spherical caps · surface area · linear approximation · quadratic approximation · numerical approximation

The area of a section of a sphere of radius R between two parallel planes a distance h apart is 2πhR. A spherical cap is the portion of the spherical surface above a horizontal slicing plane that cuts the sphere. (10 points + 3 extra credit)

  1. a) Consider a spherical cap above a plane slicing at or above the center. Find the area of the cap as a function of R and r by first finding the height h of the cap in terms of r and R (vertical distance from the slicing plane to the North Pole), then using the section area formula. (2 pts)
  2. b) Express the area formula in terms of R^2 and r/R. Use the linear and quadratic approximations to (1 + x)^{1/2} near x = 0 to find a good and an even better approximation to the area of the cap, appropriate when r/R is small. Simplify so the linear-approximation case is very familiar. (4 pts)
  3. c) A golf ball is 3 cm in diameter with 100 hemispherical dimples of diameter 3 mm. Find the area rounded to the nearest 1/100 of a square centimeter using π ≈ 3.14. Write formulas for the surface area in three cases: (i) the flat-disk approximation, (ii) the higher-order approximation from part (b), (iii) the exact formula. Evaluate each for r = 0.15 and R = 1.5 cm and find the accuracy of the approximations. (4 pts)
  4. d) (extra credit: 2-3 pts) With the diameters given, it would have been impossible for 100 dimples to be placed without overlap. Estimate the largest number of dimples that can fit on the golf ball.
解题入口

(a) h = R − sqrt(R^2 − r^2); area = 2πhR. (b) Factor out R^2 and identify a small x to expand (1+x)^{1/2} ≈ 1 + x/2 − x^2/8. (c) Apply the three formulas with the given numerical values.

完成后检查

  • (a) h = R - sqrt(R^2 - r^2)
  • (b) linear approximation yields a familiar simple formula
  • (c) numerical evaluations use π ≈ 3.14 and round to nearest 1/100
