主题梳理与示例

2. 圆环体体积（柱壳法）（Part II/2）

圆盘 \((x-b)^2+y^2\le a^2\)（\(b>a\)）绕 \(y\) 轴旋转。柱壳法：把圆盘切成竖条，距 \(y\) 轴距离 \(x\)，高 \(2\sqrt{a^2-(x-b)^2}\)，厚度 \(dx\)。体积：

\[V=\int_{b-a}^{b+a}2\pi x\cdot 2\sqrt{a^2-(x-b)^2}\,dx\]

令 \(u=x-b\)：

\[V=4\pi\int_{-a}^{a}(b+u)\sqrt{a^2-u^2}\,du=4\pi b\int_{-a}^{a}\sqrt{a^2-u^2}\,du+4\pi\int_{-a}^{a}u\sqrt{a^2-u^2}\,du\]

第二项是奇函数在对称区间积分，为 0；第一项是半圆面积 \(\pi a^2/2\)，所以：

\[V=4\pi b\cdot\frac{\pi a^2}{2}=2\pi^2 a^2 b\]

这正是「圆盘面积 \(\pi a^2\) × 圆心轨迹 \(2\pi b\)」——Pappus 定理的特例。

3. \(\tan^n x\) 的递推公式（Part II/3）

用 \(\tan^2 x=\sec^2 x-1\)：

\[\int\tan^{n+2}x\,dx=\int\tan^n x(\sec^2 x-1)\,dx=\int\tan^n x\sec^2 x\,dx-\int\tan^n x\,dx\]

第一项令 \(u=\tan x\)，\(du=\sec^2 x\,dx\)，得 \(\frac{\tan^{n+1}x}{n+1}\)。于是：

\[\int\tan^{n+2}x\,dx=\frac{\tan^{n+1}x}{n+1}-\int\tan^n x\,dx\]

这是降幂递推。第 (b) 小题求 \(\int\tan^4 x\,dx\)：用两次递推降到 \(\int\sec^2 x\,dx-\int 1\,dx\)。

4. 用部分分式积分 \(\sec x\)（Part II/4）

把 \(\sec x\) 改写：

\[\sec x=\frac{1}{\cos x}=\frac{\cos x}{\cos^2 x}=\frac{\cos x}{1-\sin^2 x}\]

令 \(u=\sin x\)，\(du=\cos x\,dx\)：

\[\int\sec x\,dx=\int\frac{du}{1-u^2}=\int\frac{du}{(1-u)(1+u)}\]

部分分式：\(\displaystyle\frac{1}{1-u^2}=\frac12\left(\frac{1}{1-u}+\frac{1}{1+u}\right)\)，积分得：

\[\frac12(-\ln|1-u|+\ln|1+u|)=\frac12\ln\left|\frac{1+u}{1-u}\right|=\frac12\ln\left|\frac{1+\sin x}{1-\sin x}\right|\]

分子分母同乘 \(1+\sin x\)，用 \(1-\sin^2 x=\cos^2 x\)，化为常见形式：

\[\int\sec x\,dx=\ln|\sec x+\tan x|+C\]

5. \(y=\cos x\) 第一拱绕 \(y\) 轴的体积（Part II/5）

第一拱从 \(x=0\) 到 \(x=\pi/2\)。柱壳法（对 \(x\) 积分）：

\[V=\int_0^{\pi/2}2\pi x\cos x\,dx\]

分部积分：\(u=x\)，\(dv=\cos x\,dx\)：

\[V=2\pi\left([x\sin x]_0^{\pi/2}-\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin x\,dx\right)=2\pi\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-1\right)=\pi^2-2\pi\]

常见坑

  • 圆环体柱壳积分限：\(x\) 从 \(b-a\) 到 \(b+a\)，令 \(u=x-b\) 化简。
  • \(\tan^n x\) 递推方向：是高次 → 低次（降幂），不是升幂。
  • \(\sec x\) 部分分式后绝对值：\(\ln|1-u|\) 不要漏绝对值。
  • 柱壳法体积的 \(2\pi\)：周长 = \(2\pi\) 乘半径，不要只写 \(\pi\)。

Problem 1 RMS voltage Trigonometric integrals · Average value · Root-mean-square

The voltage V of house current is given by V(t) = C sin(120πt) where t is time in seconds and C is a constant amplitude. The square root of the average value of V² over one period (or cycle) is called the root-mean-square voltage, abbreviated RMS. This is what the voltage meter on a house records. For house current, find the RMS in terms of C. (The peak voltage delivered to the house is ±C. The units of V² are square volts; when we take the square root again after averaging, the units become volts again.)

公式与已知条件

  • V(t) = C sin(120πt)
  • Period T = 1/60 s
  • Peak voltage ±C
解题入口

Compute the average of V² over one period using integration, then take the square root.

完成后检查

  • Check that the result has units of volts and is proportional to C.

Problem 2 Volume of a solid torus by shells Volumes of revolution · Method of shells

The solid torus is the figure obtained by rotating the disk (x-b)² + y² ≤ a² around the y-axis. Find its volume by the method of shells. (Hint: Substitute for x-b. As noted p. 229/11, the answer happens to be the area of the disk multiplied by the distance travelled by the center as it revolves.)

公式与已知条件

  • Disk: (x-b)² + y² ≤ a²
  • Rotation axis: y-axis
解题入口

Use the shell method: V = 2π∫(radius)(height)dx. Substitute u = x-b.

完成后检查

  • Verify the answer equals disk area × distance traveled by center (2πb · πa²).

Problem 3 Reduction formula for powers of tangent Trigonometric integrals · Reduction formulas

Use the substitution tan²x = sec²x - 1 to derive a reduction formula for ∫tan^(n+2)x dx and apply it.

  1. a) For any integer n ≥ 0, use the substitution tan²x = sec²x - 1 to show that ∫tan^(n+2)x dx = tan^(n+1)x/(n+1) - ∫tan^n x dx.
  2. b) Deduce a formula for ∫tan⁴x dx.

公式与已知条件

  • tan²x = sec²x - 1
  • ∫tan^(n+2)x dx = tan^(n+1)x/(n+1) - ∫tan^n x dx
解题入口

Rewrite tan^(n+2)x as tan^n x · tan²x and apply the identity tan²x = sec²x - 1.

完成后检查

  • Differentiate the result to verify it matches tan^(n+2)x.

Problem 4 Integral of secant Trigonometric integrals · Partial fractions · Substitution

Derive a formula for ∫sec x dx using substitution and partial fractions, then convert to the standard form.

  1. a) Derive a formula for ∫sec x dx by writing sec x = cos x/(1-sin²x) (verify this), and then making a substitution for sin x and using partial fractions. Your final answer must be expressed in terms of x.
  2. b) Convert the formula into the more familiar one by multiplying the fraction in the answer on both top and bottom by 1 + sin x. (Note that (1/2) ln u = ln √u.)

公式与已知条件

  • sec x = cos x/(1-sin²x)
  • (1/2) ln u = ln √u
解题入口

Let u = sin x, then decompose 1/(1-u²) into partial fractions.

完成后检查

  • Differentiate the result to verify it equals sec x.

Problem 5 Volume by shells (cosine hump) Volumes of revolution · Method of shells

Find the volume under the first hump of the function y = cos x rotated around the y-axis by the method of shells.

公式与已知条件

  • y = cos x
  • First hump: 0 ≤ x ≤ π/2
解题入口

Use the shell method: V = 2π∫x·cos x dx from x = 0 to π/2.
