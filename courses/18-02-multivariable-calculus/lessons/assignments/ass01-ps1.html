Problem Set 1: Vectors & Dot Product (向量与点积)

从立方体中的正四面体到三角形的垂心，用点积的代数定义与几何意义搭建向量直觉。

发布：2007-09-06 截止：2007-09-13 难度：入门

学习导读

这份作业是进入 18.02 的第一道门槛。它的核心任务是让你从「向量是有方向的箭头」这个直觉，过渡到「用分量做代数运算，再用几何定理读出方向关系」的双重语言。点积 (dot product) 是这把钥匙：代数上它只是分量乘积之和，几何上它却编码了长度、夹角与正交性。

目标：熟练运用 \(\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B}=a_1b_1+a_2b_2+a_3b_3\) 与 \(\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B}=|\vec{A}||\vec{B}|\cos\theta\)，完成长度、夹角、投影、正交判定等基本计算，并在几何情境（四面体、三角形垂心、面积向量）中灵活切换代数与几何两种视角。

知识要点回顾

向量基本运算

向量 \(\vec{A}=\langle a_1,a_2,a_3\rangle\) 的长度 (length) 由勾股定理给出：

\[|\vec{A}|=\sqrt{a_1^2+a_2^2+a_3^2}.\]

标量乘法 \(c\vec{A}\) 按分量逐个伸缩；向量加法 \(\vec{A}+\vec{B}\) 按分量逐个相加，几何上对应首尾相接或平行四边形法则。

点积的两种定义

代数定义：\(\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B}=a_1b_1+a_2b_2+a_3b_3\)。

几何定义：\(\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B}=|\vec{A}||\vec{B}|\cos\theta\)，其中 \(\theta\) 为两向量夹角。

二者等价，由余弦定理证明。这个等价是整个作业的核心：代数用来算，几何用来想。

从点积读出几何信息

  • 夹角：\(\displaystyle \cos\theta=\frac{\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B}}{|\vec{A}||\vec{B}|}\)。若点积为正则锐角，负则钝角。
  • 正交 (orthogonal)：\(\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B}=0\) ⇔ 两向量垂直（或其中一个是零向量）。
  • 投影 (projection)：\(\vec{A}\) 在 \(\vec{B}\) 方向上的标量分量为 \(\displaystyle \frac{\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B}}{|\vec{B}|}\)，向量分量为 \(\displaystyle \operatorname{proj}_{\vec{B}}\vec{A}=\frac{\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B}}{|\vec{B}|^2}\,\vec{B}\)。

核心思想：只要问题涉及「是否垂直」「夹角多大」「沿某方向走多远」，就应当条件反射地想到点积。它是把几何问题翻译成代数计算的通用接口。

题目概览与解题策略

Problem 1：立方体中的正四面体 (Thursday, 5 pts)

单位立方体顶点 \((\pm1,\pm1,\pm1)\) 中，从 \((1,1,1)\) 出发选取四个两两不相邻的顶点，就得到一个内接正四面体 (regular tetrahedron)。关键观察：所选顶点之间恰好相差两个坐标符号。

  • (a) 验证边长：对任意两顶点 \(\vec{p},\vec{q}\)，计算 \(|\vec{p}-\vec{q}|\) 并确认为常数。
  • (b) 键角 (bond angle)：中心 \((0,0,0)\) 到两个顶点的向量用点积求夹角，\(\cos\theta = -1/3\)，约为 \(109.5^\circ\)。
  • (c) 相邻边 vs. 对边：相邻边共用一个顶点，对边则不共用；用点积分别计算，再用对称性解释。
  • (d) 面的面积：可用叉积 \(|\vec{B}\times\vec{C}|/2\)（周五学过），也可用底乘高除以二。

Problem 2：三角形的垂心 (Thursday, 3 pts)

设 \(P_1,P_2,P_3\) 为三角形顶点，\(P\) 为平面上任一点，\(\vec{v}_i=\overrightarrow{P_iP}\)。点 \(P\) 在从 \(P_1\) 出发的高线上 ⇔ \(\vec{v}_1\) 与对边向量 \(\overrightarrow{P_2P_3}\) 垂直。

  • (a) 用点积写出「\(P\) 在从 \(P_1\) 出发的高线上」的代数条件：\(\vec{v}_1\cdot\overrightarrow{P_2P_3}=0\)。
  • (b) 设 \(P\) 是两条高线的交点，证明三个点积 \(\vec{v}_1\cdot\vec{v}_2=\vec{v}_1\cdot\vec{v}_3=\vec{v}_2\cdot\vec{v}_3\)。
  • (c) 再由 (b) 的等式推出 \(P\) 也在第三条高线上 ⇒ 三高共点，即垂心 (orthocenter)。

注意：题目中的「高线」是整条过顶点且垂直于对边的直线，不只是垂线段。因此条件应是 \(\vec{v}_1\cdot(\overrightarrow{P_2P_3})=0\)，而不是 \(P\) 落在对边线段上。

Problem 3：四面体面积向量之和为零 (Friday, 3 pts)

设四面体一个顶点出发的三条棱向量为 \(\vec{A},\vec{B},\vec{C}\)。每个面的外法向面积向量等于相邻两面叉积的一半。

核心思想：不必引入坐标系。设从公共顶点出发的三条棱为 \(\vec{A},\vec{B},\vec{C}\)，则四个面的有向面积向量分别是 \(\frac12\vec{A}\times\vec{B}\)、\(\frac12\vec{B}\times\vec{C}\)、\(\frac12\vec{C}\times\vec{A}\)，以及对面的 \(\frac12(\vec{A}-\vec{B})\times(\vec{C}-\vec{B})\)（注意方向朝外）。四项相加后因叉积反对称性 \(\vec{u}\times\vec{v}=-\vec{v}\times\vec{u}\) 而消为零。

Problem 4：正交矩阵 (Tuesday, 9 pts)

满足 \(AA^T=I\) 的方阵叫正交矩阵 (orthogonal matrix)，它保持向量长度与夹角。二阶情形只有两类：旋转 (rotation) 与反射 (reflection)。

  • (a) 计算 \(A_\theta\hat{\imath},A_\theta\hat{\jmath}\) 并画图。
  • (b) 用和角公式验证 \(A_{\theta_1}A_{\theta_2}=A_{\theta_1+\theta_2}\) —— 旋转的复合仍是旋转。
  • (c) 验证 \(A_\theta^{-1}=A_\theta^T=A_{-\theta}\)，说明逆时针旋转 \(\theta\) 的逆是顺时针旋转 \(\theta\)。
  • (d) 列出所有第一列为 \((-1/\sqrt2,\ast)^T\) 的正交矩阵：四个符号组合。
  • (e) 把每个矩阵作用于字母 F，观察 \(\det A=+1\) 时为旋转（F 只是转），\(\det A=-1\) 时为反射（F 镜像翻转）。

注意：正交矩阵的行列式只能是 \(\pm1\)。\(\det=+1\) 对应旋转，\(\det=-1\) 对应反射。很多同学会忘记反射那一半。

Problem 5：矩阵与营养计算 (Tuesday, 7 pts)

矩阵 \(M\) (4×3) 把糕点数量向量映射为配料向量；矩阵 \(N\) (3×4) 把配料向量映射为营养素向量。

  • (a) \(MX\) 的各个分量是面粉、糖、鸡蛋、黄油的克数。
  • (b) 总营养值为 \(N(MX)=(NM)X\)，即 \(3\times3\) 矩阵乘以糕点向量。
  • (c) 由 \(Y=(NM)X\) 得 \(X=(NM)^{-1}Y\)，用计算器求逆矩阵。
  • (d) 代入每日推荐量，得到的解会出现负值 —— 说明仅靠这三种糕点无法同时满足三种营养目标。

核心思想：矩阵乘法对应线性映射的复合。这里 \(M\) 把「糕点」变「配料」，\(N\) 把「配料」变「营养」，乘积 \(NM\) 一步完成两个过程。

常见错误

  • 点积 vs. 叉积混淆：点积给出标量，叉积给出向量。判断垂直用点积为零，求法向量用叉积。
  • 方向向量未归一化就做投影：标量投影公式是 \(\frac{\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B}}{|\vec{B}|}\)，若忘记除以 \(|\vec{B}|\) 会得到错误结果。
  • 四面体顶点选错：正四面体的四个顶点在立方体中应是两两之间相差两个坐标符号的顶点，而不是任意四个。
  • 正交矩阵只写旋转：行列式为 \(-1\) 的反射也是正交矩阵，常被遗漏。
  • 矩阵乘法的顺序：\(NM\) 与 \(MN\) 一般不同，因为 \(N\) 是 \(3\times4\)、\(M\) 是 \(4\times3\)，只有 \(NM\)（\(3\times3\)）才有意义。

本作业要点

点积双视角

代数定义用来计算，几何定义用来读出夹角与正交性。

投影与分量

沿任意方向的分量 \(\frac{\vec{A}\cdot\vec{B}}{|\vec{B}|}\) 是把向量分解的基本工具。

面积向量

四面体各面的外法向面积向量之和为零，是 Stokes 定理的离散雏形。

正交矩阵

矩阵复合

\(NM\) 把「糕点 → 配料 → 营养」两步并为一步。

Problem B-2 Altitudes and orthocenter Altitudes · Orthocenter · Dot product perpendicularity

Consider a triangle in the plane with vertices P1, P2, and P3.

  1. a) Let v1, v2 and v3 be the vectors in the plane from the points P1, P2 and P3 respectively to a point P. Express in terms of the dot product and these three vectors the condition that P is on the altitude of the triangle P1P2P3 from the vertex P1. (By altitude we mean the entire line through a vertex perpendicular to the opposite side, not just the segment from the vertex to the side.)
  2. b) Assume that P is at the intersection of the altitudes from P1 and P2. Show that v1·v2 = v1·v3 = v2·v3.
  3. c) Under the assumptions in (b), show that P is also on the altitude from P3. (Hence all three altitudes meet in one point, called the orthocenter.)
解题入口

Use dot product to express perpendicularity of vectors.

完成后检查

Problem B-4 Orthogonal matrices Orthogonal matrices · Rotations · Reflections · Determinant

Orthogonal matrices are matrices A that satisfy the identity A A^T = I (I is the identity matrix). An equivalent definition is that A^T A = I because the left and right inverses of a square matrix are the same (see 1G-9b). The equation A A^T = I says that the rows of A are perpendicular to each other and of unit length, whereas the equation A^T A = I says that the columns of A are perpendicular to each other and of unit length. The geometric significance of orthogonal matrices is that multiplication by an orthogonal matrix preserves lengths of vectors and the absolute values of angles between them: |Av| = |v| and |∠(Av, Aw)| = |∠(v, w)|. There are two types of orthogonal matrices, rotations and reflections.

  1. a) In 2-dimensional space, rotations are given by A_θ = [[cos θ, -sin θ],[sin θ, cos θ]]. Find u = A_θ î and v = A_θ ĵ, and draw a picture of u and v for θ = π/4.
  2. b) Use the addition formulas for sine and cosine to deduce that A_{θ1} A_{θ2} = A_{θ1+θ2}. Say in words what this matrix formula means about rotations.
  3. c) Calculate A_θ^{-1}, and use this to verify that A_θ A_θ^T = I (in other words, rotations are orthogonal matrices). Also verify that A_θ^{-1} = A_{-θ}, and give a geometric reason why this property holds.
  4. d) Find the four orthogonal 2 × 2 matrices with first entry a_{11} = -1/√2. Hint: try different signs. (See 1F-9 and 1F-10).
  5. e) Next to each of the matrices in your list in part (d), draw what the matrix does to the letter F in the plane. Explain how the sign of the determinant of the matrix is related to the appearance of the transformed F.

公式与已知条件

  • A_θ = [[cos θ, -sin θ],[sin θ, cos θ]]
  • Preservation: |Av| = |v|, |∠(Av, Aw)| = |∠(v, w)|
解题入口

Use sine/cosine addition formulas; try different sign combinations for the entries.

完成后检查

  • Verify A_θ A_θ^T = I.
  • Verify A_θ^{-1} = A_{-θ}.

Problem B-5 Pastry nutrition matrix model Matrix multiplication · Linear systems · Nutrition modeling

Cookies, doughnuts, and croissants contain essentially the same ingredients (flour, sugar, egg, butter) but in different proportions. For example, it takes 22 grams of flour to make a cookie, versus 40 g for a doughnut and 50 g for a croissant. The compositions of the various pastries can be encoded in a 4 × 3 matrix M = [[22,40,50],[18,10,3],[5,14,5],[10,10,22]], where the entries in the i-th row represent the amounts of ingredient i (i = 1 for flour, 2 for sugar, 3 for egg, 4 for butter) required to make the various types of pastries.

  1. a) Consider an assortment of x1 cookies, x2 doughnuts and x3 croissants, and form the column vector X = [x1, x2, x3]^T. What do the entries of the vector M X correspond to?
  2. b) Each of the four ingredients has a specific nutritional value: for example, 100 grams of flour contain 10 g of protein, 76 g of carbohydrates, and 1 g of fat. Proceeding similarly for all four ingredients, we can build a 3 × 4 matrix N = [[0.10,0,0.13,0],[0.76,1.00,0.01,0],[0.01,0,0.10,0.82]], where the entries in the i-th column represent the proportions of protein, carbohydrates and fat in ingredient i. Give a matrix formula for the total nutritional value of the assortment of pastries considered in (a). (Keep your answer in symbolic form, do not evaluate numerically.)
  3. c) Give a matrix formula expressing the numbers xi of pastries of each type which will add up to y1 g of protein, y2 g of carbohydrates, and y3 g of fat. Express your answer in the form X = A Y, and give both a formula for A and numerical values for its entries (use either a calculator or Matlab).
  4. d) The recommended daily amounts of protein, carbohydrates and fat for a 2000 calorie diet are 50, 300, and 65 grams respectively. If you wanted to follow those guidelines while eating only cookies, doughnuts, and croissants, how many pastries of each type should you eat daily? What is wrong with your answer? Explain.

公式与已知条件

  • M = [[22,40,50],[18,10,3],[5,14,5],[10,10,22]]
  • N = [[0.10,0,0.13,0],[0.76,1.00,0.01,0],[0.01,0,0.10,0.82]]
  • Daily targets: protein 50 g, carbohydrates 300 g, fat 65 g
解题入口

Use symbolic matrix multiplication; for X = A Y compute A using a calculator or Matlab.

完成后检查

  • Describe what M X entries represent (ingredient totals).
  • Interpret any negative or impractical pastry counts.
