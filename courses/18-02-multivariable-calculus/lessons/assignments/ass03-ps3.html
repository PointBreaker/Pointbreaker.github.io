知识要点回顾

矩阵乘法与逆

设 \(A\) 为 \(n\times n\) 方阵。若存在 \(A^{-1}\) 使得 \(AA^{-1}=A^{-1}A=I\)，则 \(A\) 可逆，线性方程组 \(A\vec{x}=\vec{b}\) 的解为 \(\vec{x}=A^{-1}\vec{b}\)。

余子式法求逆：\(\displaystyle A^{-1}=\frac{1}{\det A}\,\operatorname{adj}(A)\)，其中 \(\operatorname{adj}(A)\) 是余子式矩阵的转置（伴随矩阵）。

参数方程

直线过点 \(P_0\) 沿方向 \(\vec{v}\) 的参数方程：\(\vec{r}(t)=P_0+t\vec{v}\)。

对参数曲线 \(\vec{r}(t)=\langle x(t),y(t),z(t)\rangle\)，速度 \(\vec{v}(t)=\vec{r}'(t)\)，加速度 \(\vec{a}(t)=\vec{r}''(t)\)。

偏导数与线性逼近

函数 \(w=f(x,y)\) 的全微分：

\[dw = f_x\,dx + f_y\,dy.\]

当 \(\Delta x,\Delta y\) 很小时，可用线性近似：

\[\Delta w \approx f_x\,\Delta x + f_y\,\Delta y.\]

核心思想：矩阵是线性变换的具体表示。透视投影、线性方程组求解、参数曲线的切向量，本质上都是「线性关系」在不同几何场景中的化身。

题目概览与解题策略

Problem 1：透视投影 (immediately, 8 pts)

屏幕为 \(yz\) 平面，观察者眼睛位于 \(E(2,0,0)\)。对空间一点 \(P(x_0,y_0,z_0)\)，求其在屏幕上的投影点 \(Q(y,z)\)。

  • (a) 过 \(E\) 与 \(P\) 的直线与 \(yz\) 平面的交点。利用参数方程：\(E+t(P-E)\) 在 \(x=0\) 时求得 \(t\)，代回得 \(y,z\)。
  • (b) 线段的投影仍是线段（直线与平面的交保持线性）。
  • (c) 给具体端点，直接套用 (a) 的公式。
  • (d) 鸟的飞行轨迹投影；当 \(t\to\infty\) 时趋于消失点 (vanishing point)，即 \(E\) 到 \(P_0P_1\) 方向的无穷远点投影。
  • (e) 栅栏遮挡问题：找出被栅栏遮挡的点所在的平面，确定参数范围。

Problem 2：数值逼近 (Tuesday, 4 pts)

设 \(w=xy=e^{y\ln x}\)。

  • (a) 用全微分给出 \(\Delta w\approx y\,x^{y-1}\Delta x + x^y\ln x\,\Delta y\)，估算 \(1.98^{2.01}\)。
  • (b) 比较 \(\frac{\partial w}{\partial x}\Delta x\) 与 \(\frac{\partial w}{\partial y}\Delta y\) 的大小，判断哪个变量更敏感。

注意：线性近似的精度依赖 \(\Delta x,\Delta y\) 足够小。若增量较大，近似值可能偏离真实值较远。

Problem 3：等高线图与偏导数 (Tuesday, 7 pts)

给定 \(f(x,y)=x^3-xy^2-4x^2+3x+x^2y\) 的等高线图。

  • (a) 从等高线走向判断 \(f_x,f_y\) 在各点处的符号：往东走若登高则 \(f_x>0\)，往北走若登高则 \(f_y>0\)。
  • (b) 解析计算偏导数并与 (a) 对照。
  • (c) 用课程网页的 applet 验证。
  • (d) 找临界点（\(f_x=f_y=0\)），描述附近等高线形状与各方向升降。

核心思想：等高线图是三维曲面的「俯视图」。偏导数的符号告诉我们往哪个方向走函数值增加，临界点则是「山顶」「山谷」或「鞍部」。

常见错误

  • 矩阵乘法顺序：\(AB\neq BA\)，尤其在复合变换中顺序至关重要。
  • 忘记转置伴随矩阵：余子式法求逆时，余子式矩阵必须转置得到伴随矩阵。
  • 参数方程求导漏掉链式法则：对 \(x(t),y(t),z(t)\) 分别求导，不要忘记复合函数情形。
  • 线性近似的适用范围：当 \(\Delta x,\Delta y\) 不够小时，线性近似可能严重偏离真实值。
  • 等高线符号判读：往东走 = \(x\) 增加方向，往北走 = \(y\) 增加方向，不要与「上北下南」的地图直觉混淆。

Problem 1 3D computer graphics projection Parametric projection · Vanishing points · Occlusion by a fence/plane

In 3D computer graphics, one needs to represent 3-dimensional objects on a plane screen, by drawing a given point P at the place where the line from P to the eye meets the screen. Suppose that the screen is the yz-plane, and the eye is at E: (2, 0, 0).

  1. a) At what point Q: (y, z) in the yz-plane should one represent the point P: (x0, y0, z0)? (Express y and z in terms of the coordinates of P. Assume that x0 < 2. Why is this assumption legitimate?)
  2. b) What does the image on the screen of a line segment in space look like? (Justify your answer.)
  3. c) A line segment connects P0: (-1, -3, 1) to P1: (-2, 4, 6). What is drawn on the screen?
  4. d) A bird leaves from P0 at time t = 0, and flies in a straight line at constant speed in such a way that it passes through P1 at time t = 1. What does the trajectory of the bird (for t >= 0) look like on the screen? Show that, as t tends to infinity, the trajectory on the screen tends to a limit point (the "vanishing point"), and give its coordinates.
  5. e) In fact, part of the trajectory of the bird is hidden by a vertical fence erected in front of the observer. The fence lies in the plane x = 1, and its top is at the altitude z = 1. What portion of the trajectory is hidden? (Hint: observe that the points hidden by the fence are exactly those which lie below a certain plane passing through E.)

公式与已知条件

  • Eye E = (2, 0, 0); screen is the yz-plane (x = 0)
  • P0 = (-1, -3, 1), P1 = (-2, 4, 6)
  • Fence: plane x = 1, top at z = 1
解题入口

Parametrize the line through E and P, then intersect with x = 0 to find Q. For the bird, parametrize position as a function of t then project. For the fence, find the plane through E and the top edge of the fence, then determine which projected points lie below it.

完成后检查

  • Verify x0 < 2 ensures the point is in front of the eye so projection is well-defined. Confirm the limit point in (d) is the projection of the direction vector's point at infinity.

Problem 2 Differentials and sensitivity of w = xy Differentials · Tangent approximation · Sensitivity analysis

Let w = xy (= e^(y ln x)).

  1. a) Give an approximate formula for the small change Δw in the value of w produced by small changes Δx and Δy in the values of x and y. Use this to calculate an approximate value of 1.98^2.01. Compare your answer with the exact value.
  2. b) Starting from 2^2 = 4, is the value of xy more sensitive to small changes in x or in the exponent y? (Justify your answer.)

公式与已知条件

  • w = x^y = e^(y ln x)
  • Differential: dw = (y x^(y-1)) dx + (x^y ln x) dy
  • Base point for (b): x = 2, y = 2, w = 4
解题入口

Compute partial derivatives of x^y, form the differential dw, then plug in the small Δx, Δy. For (b), compare the magnitudes of the two partial derivative terms at (2,2).

完成后检查

  • Confirm the approximation 1.98^2.01 is close to the exact value. In (b), the comparison should be between |y x^(y-1) Δx| and |x^y ln x Δy|.

Problem 3 Contour plot and partial derivatives Contour plots · Partial derivatives from contour plots · Critical points · Level curves

The figure below is the contour plot of a function of two variables f(x, y), for x and y ranging between 0 to 2 (scale: 1 unit = 5 cm; spacing between contour levels: 0.2).

  1. a) Use the contour plot to determine whether fx and fy are > 0, = 0, or < 0 at the point (1, 1.5). Same question at the point (1.2, 0.6)?
  2. b) The function plotted on the figure is f(x, y) = x^3 - x y^2 - 4x^2 + 3x + x^2 y. Calculate the actual values of the partial derivatives at (1, 1.5) and (1.2, 0.6).
  3. c) Use the applet on the course web page (http://math.mit.edu/18.02/applets/) to plot the graph of f corresponding to this contour plot. Read briefly the directions on the web page, and familiarize yourself with the controls. In the "Level curves" view, see how the slider in the lower-left corner moves the highlighted contour line on both plots, and experiment with it in order to perfect your understanding of the contour plot. In the "Partial derivatives" view, see how clicking on a point in the small contour plot displays the x- and y-slices of the graph through that point. Finally, use the applet to check the answers you found in (b).
  4. d) Use the applet to find two points where fx = fy = 0, and give their approximate coordinates. What happens to the level curves of f through these points? For each of the two points, describe what happens when you move towards North, South, East, West: does the value of f go up, down, or does it stay exactly the same?

公式与已知条件

  • f(x, y) = x^3 - x y^2 - 4x^2 + 3x + x^2 y
  • fx = 3x^2 - y^2 - 8x + 3 + 2xy

  • Contour spacing: 0.2; scale 1 unit = 5 cm
解题入口

Use the contour plot to read whether moving in +x or +y crosses to higher or lower contour levels. Then verify by differentiating f. Use the applet to locate where both partials vanish and examine the local level curve structure.

完成后检查

  • The signs from (a) should match the computed signs in (b). Critical points satisfy fx = fy = 0.
