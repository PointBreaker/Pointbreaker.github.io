Problem Set 10: Line Integrals (线积分)

进入向量微积分的核心：向量场沿曲线的积分、功与通量的计算、梯度场与路径无关性，以及判定保守场的准则 \(P_y=Q_x\)。这是第三单元 Unit IIIa 的综合习题，Exam 3 的直接前奏。

发布：2007-10-25 截止：2007-11-01 范围：Lectures 19, 20, 21 难度：中等 → 较难

目标

完成本次习题后，你应该能够：对参数化的曲线 \(C\) 计算向量场 \(\vec{F}=\langle M,N\rangle\) 的线积分 \(\int_C \vec{F}\cdot d\vec{r} = \int_C M\,dx+N\,dy\)；用几何方法 \(\int_C \vec{F}\cdot\hat{T}\,ds\) 处理特殊情形；识别梯度场并用 FTC for line integrals \(\int_C \nabla f\cdot d\vec{r}=f(P_1)-f(P_0)\) 简化计算；用准则 \(P_y=Q_x\) 判断保守性，并用两种方法求势函数。

核心思想：线积分 \(\int_C \vec{F}\cdot d\vec{r}\) 的物理意义是力 \(\vec{F}\) 沿路径 \(C\) 做的功。Auroux 在第 19 讲强调：无论用参数化 \(\vec{r}(t)\)、微分形式 \(M\,dx+N\,dy\)、还是几何形式 \(\vec{F}\cdot\hat{T}\,ds\)，结果都是同一个数——选择最简便的那条路。

核心知识要点

1. 线积分的定义

设 \(C\) 是以 \(\vec{r}(t)=\langle x(t),y(t)\rangle\)（\(t_0\le t\le t_1\)）参数化的曲线，\(\vec{F}=\langle M(x,y),N(x,y)\rangle\) 是向量场。线积分：

\[ \int_C \vec{F}\cdot d\vec{r} = \int_{t_0}^{t_1} \vec{F}(\vec{r}(t))\cdot\vec{r}'(t)\,dt = \int_C M\,dx + N\,dy. \]

关键：把 \(x,y,dx,dy\) 全部用参数 \(t\) 表示。Auroux 的例子：\(\vec{F}=\langle -y,x\rangle\)，\(C: x=t,\ y=t^2,\ 0\le t\le1\)：

\[ \int_C -y\,dx + x\,dy = \int_0^1 (-t^2\cdot 1 + t\cdot 2t)\,dt = \int_0^1 t^2\,dt = \frac13. \]

注意：\(M\,dx+N\,dy\) 不是对 \(x\) 和对 \(y\) 的两个独立积分，而是沿曲线 \(C\) 的线积分。必须先参数化，再代入计算。

2. 几何方法

因为 \(d\vec{r} = \hat{T}\,ds\)（\(\hat{T}\) 是单位切向量，\(ds\) 是弧长微元），所以：

\[ \int_C \vec{F}\cdot d\vec{r} = \int_C (\vec{F}\cdot\hat{T})\,ds. \]

当 \(\vec{F}\) 与 \(\hat{T}\) 的夹角简单时，这比参数化更直观。例如 \(\vec{F}=\langle -y,x\rangle\) 沿逆时针单位圆：\(\vec{F}\) 与 \(\hat{T}\) 平行且同向，\(|\vec{F}|=1\)，所以 \(\int_C \vec{F}\cdot\hat{T}\,ds = \int_C 1\cdot ds = 2\pi\)。

3. 线积分的微基本定理 (FTC for line integrals)

若 \(\vec{F}=\nabla f = \langle f_x,f_y\rangle\)（即 \(\vec{F}\) 是梯度场，\(f\) 是势函数），则：

\[ \int_C \nabla f\cdot d\vec{r} = f(P_1) - f(P_0), \]

其中 \(P_0,P_1\) 是 \(C\) 的起止点。与路径无关！

证明：\(\displaystyle \int_C \nabla f\cdot d\vec{r} = \int_{t_0}^{t_1} \frac{d}{dt}f(x(t),y(t))\,dt = f(P_1)-f(P_0)\)。

4. 路径无关性、保守场与等价命题

对梯度场，以下四个命题等价：

  1. \(\vec{F}\) 是保守场：\(\displaystyle\oint_C \vec{F}\cdot d\vec{r}=0\) 对任意闭路 \(C\)
  2. 线积分路径无关：同一端点的任何两条路径积分相等
  3. \(\vec{F}\) 是梯度场：\(\vec{F}=\nabla f\)
  4. \(M\,dx+N\,dy\) 是恰当微分：存在 \(f\) 使 \(df=M\,dx+N\,dy\)

重要反例：\(\vec{F}=\langle -y,x\rangle\) 不是梯度场——沿单位圆逆时针的积分是 \(2\pi\neq0\)。物理上，这个场对应旋转运动，做功与路径有关。

5. 保守场的判定准则

若 \(\vec{F}=\langle M,N\rangle\) 在整个平面（或单连通区域）上连续可微，则：

\[ \vec{F}\text{ 是保守场} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \frac{\partial N}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial M}{\partial y}. \]

定义二维旋度 (curl)：

\[ \operatorname{curl}\vec{F} = N_x - M_y. \]

保守场 ⇔ curl = 0。

例子：\(\vec{F}=\langle 4x^2+8xy,\ 3y^2+4x2\rangle\)，则 \(N_x=8x=M_y\)，是保守场。

核心思想：\(\operatorname{curl}\vec{F}\) 衡量场的旋转程度。均匀平移场 \(\vec{F}=\langle a,b\rangle\)、径向膨胀场 \(\vec{F}=\langle x,y\rangle\) 的 curl 都为零；而旋转场 \(\vec{F}=\langle -y,x\rangle\) 的 curl = 2（对应单位角速度）。

6. 求势函数的两种方法

已知 \(\vec{F}=\langle M,N\rangle\) 是保守场，求 \(f\) 使 \(\nabla f=\vec{F}\)：

方法一（反向 FTC）：选一条从 \((0,0)\) 到 \((x_1,y_1)\) 的简单路径（如先沿 \(x\) 轴再垂直向上）：

\[ f(x_1,y_1) = f(0,0) + \int_0^{x_1} M(x,0)\,dx + \int_0^{y_1} N(x_1,y)\,dy. \]

方法二（反导数法）：

  1. 对 \(f_x=M\) 关于 \(x\) 积分：\(f = \int M\,dx + g(y)\)
  2. 对 \(y\) 求偏导得 \(f_y = \partial/\partial y(\int M\,dx) + g'(y)\)，与 \(N\) 比较
  3. 解出 \(g'(y)\) 并积分

典型题型与解题策略

1

直接计算线积分

代表：PS10 中给定参数化曲线的题目；分段路径（如扇形的三条边）。

策略：参数化 → 代入 \(\vec{F}\) 和 \(d\vec{r}\) → 化为单变量积分。对分段路径逐段计算再求和。几何法（\(\vec{F}\cdot\hat{T}\)）在特殊情形下更快捷。

2

识别梯度场并用 FTC

代表：PS10 中形如 \(\int_C \nabla f\cdot d\vec{r}\) 或可识别为梯度场的题目。

策略：验证 \(N_x=M_y\) → 确认是保守场 → 找势函数 \(f\) → 用 \(f(P_1)-f(P_0)\) 得结果，省去参数化。

3

判断保守性并求势函数

代表：含参数的向量场题（如"对哪些 \(a,b\) 是保守场"）；求势函数。

策略：令 \(N_x=M_y\) 解出参数。求 \(f\) 时用方法一（简单路径）或方法二（反导数），互为验证。

4

比较不同路径的积分

代表：同一向量场沿不同路径从 \(P_0\) 到 \(P_1\) 的积分比较。

策略：若场是保守场，所有路径积分相等，选最简单的路径（如折线）计算。若不是保守场，必须按指定路径参数化计算。

常见错误

把 \(M\,dx+N\,dy\) 当两个独立积分：线积分是沿曲线的积分，必须通过参数化把 \(x,y,dx,dy\) 都写成同一个变量的函数。直接"对 \(x\) 积 \(M\)、对 \(y\) 积 \(N\)"会得到错误结果。

忽略方向：线积分与路径方向有关：\(\displaystyle\int_{-C} \vec{F}\cdot d\vec{r} = -\int_C \vec{F}\cdot d\vec{r}\)。闭路的方向（逆时针 vs 顺时针）影响正负号。

盲目用 FTC：FTC for line integrals 只对梯度场成立。\(\vec{F}=\langle -y,x\rangle\) 沿单位圆积分是 \(2\pi\) 而非 0——必须先验证 \(N_x=M_y\) 才能用 FTC。

势函数遗漏积分常数：方法一中 \(f(0,0)\) 是任意常数；方法二中 \(g(y)\) 的常数项。势函数不唯一，但任意两个势函数只差一个常数。

Problem B-4 Orthogonal matrices Orthogonal matrices · Rotations · Reflections · Determinant

Orthogonal matrices are matrices A that satisfy the identity A A^T = I (I is the identity matrix). An equivalent definition is that A^T A = I because the left and right inverses of a square matrix are the same (see 1G-9b). The equation A A^T = I says that the rows of A are perpendicular to each other and of unit length, whereas the equation A^T A = I says that the columns of A are perpendicular to each other and of unit length. The geometric significance of orthogonal matrices is that multiplication by an orthogonal matrix preserves lengths of vectors and the absolute values of angles between them: |Av| = |v| and |∠(Av, Aw)| = |∠(v, w)|. There are two types of orthogonal matrices, rotations and reflections.

  1. a) In 2-dimensional space, rotations are given by A_θ = [[cos θ, -sin θ],[sin θ, cos θ]]. Find u = A_θ î and v = A_θ ĵ, and draw a picture of u and v for θ = π/4.
  2. b) Use the addition formulas for sine and cosine to deduce that A_{θ1} A_{θ2} = A_{θ1+θ2}. Say in words what this matrix formula means about rotations.
  3. c) Calculate A_θ^{-1}, and use this to verify that A_θ A_θ^T = I (in other words, rotations are orthogonal matrices). Also verify that A_θ^{-1} = A_{-θ}, and give a geometric reason why this property holds.
  4. d) Find the four orthogonal 2 × 2 matrices with first entry a_{11} = -1/√2. Hint: try different signs. (See 1F-9 and 1F-10).
  5. e) Next to each of the matrices in your list in part (d), draw what the matrix does to the letter F in the plane. Explain how the sign of the determinant of the matrix is related to the appearance of the transformed F.

公式与已知条件

  • A_θ = [[cos θ, -sin θ],[sin θ, cos θ]]
  • Preservation: |Av| = |v|, |∠(Av, Aw)| = |∠(v, w)|
解题入口

Use sine/cosine addition formulas; try different sign combinations for the entries.

完成后检查

  • Verify A_θ A_θ^T = I.
  • Verify A_θ^{-1} = A_{-θ}.

Problem B-5 Pastry nutrition matrix model Matrix multiplication · Linear systems · Nutrition modeling

Cookies, doughnuts, and croissants contain essentially the same ingredients (flour, sugar, egg, butter) but in different proportions. For example, it takes 22 grams of flour to make a cookie, versus 40 g for a doughnut and 50 g for a croissant. The compositions of the various pastries can be encoded in a 4 × 3 matrix M = [[22,40,50],[18,10,3],[5,14,5],[10,10,22]], where the entries in the i-th row represent the amounts of ingredient i (i = 1 for flour, 2 for sugar, 3 for egg, 4 for butter) required to make the various types of pastries.

  1. a) Consider an assortment of x1 cookies, x2 doughnuts and x3 croissants, and form the column vector X = [x1, x2, x3]^T. What do the entries of the vector M X correspond to?
  2. b) Each of the four ingredients has a specific nutritional value: for example, 100 grams of flour contain 10 g of protein, 76 g of carbohydrates, and 1 g of fat. Proceeding similarly for all four ingredients, we can build a 3 × 4 matrix N = [[0.10,0,0.13,0],[0.76,1.00,0.01,0],[0.01,0,0.10,0.82]], where the entries in the i-th column represent the proportions of protein, carbohydrates and fat in ingredient i. Give a matrix formula for the total nutritional value of the assortment of pastries considered in (a). (Keep your answer in symbolic form, do not evaluate numerically.)
  3. c) Give a matrix formula expressing the numbers xi of pastries of each type which will add up to y1 g of protein, y2 g of carbohydrates, and y3 g of fat. Express your answer in the form X = A Y, and give both a formula for A and numerical values for its entries (use either a calculator or Matlab).
  4. d) The recommended daily amounts of protein, carbohydrates and fat for a 2000 calorie diet are 50, 300, and 65 grams respectively. If you wanted to follow those guidelines while eating only cookies, doughnuts, and croissants, how many pastries of each type should you eat daily? What is wrong with your answer? Explain.

公式与已知条件

  • M = [[22,40,50],[18,10,3],[5,14,5],[10,10,22]]
  • N = [[0.10,0,0.13,0],[0.76,1.00,0.01,0],[0.01,0,0.10,0.82]]
  • Daily targets: protein 50 g, carbohydrates 300 g, fat 65 g
解题入口

Use symbolic matrix multiplication; for X = A Y compute A using a calculator or Matlab.

完成后检查

  • Describe what M X entries represent (ingredient totals).
  • Interpret any negative or impractical pastry counts.
